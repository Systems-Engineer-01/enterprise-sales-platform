# Product Goal

## Goal

Build a secure, maintainable and scalable enterprise platform
that centralizes product, inventory, customer, supplier,
purchasing and sales management.

## Business Outcome

Provide organizations with a centralized source of operational
information that improves traceability, inventory visibility,
sales management and decision-making.

## MVP Outcome

A user with the appropriate permissions must be able to:

1. Authenticate.
2. Manage authorized business information.
3. Manage products.
4. Manage customers and suppliers.
5. Manage inventory.
6. Register purchases.
7. Register sales.
8. Review operational information.
9. Maintain an auditable record of important operations.
