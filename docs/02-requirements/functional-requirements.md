# Functional Requirements

## 1. Purpose

Define the functional capabilities that the Enterprise Sales
Platform must provide.

---

## 2. Authentication

### FR-AUTH-001 — User Authentication

The system shall allow registered users to authenticate using
valid credentials.

### FR-AUTH-002 — Credential Validation

The system shall validate the credentials provided by the user.

### FR-AUTH-003 — Authentication Failure

The system shall reject invalid authentication attempts without
revealing sensitive authentication information.

### FR-AUTH-004 — Secure Session

The system shall establish a secure authenticated session after
successful authentication.

---

## 3. User and Access Management

### FR-USER-001 — User Registration

The system shall allow authorized administrators to register users.

### FR-USER-002 — User Management

The system shall allow authorized administrators to create,
consult, update and deactivate users.

### FR-USER-003 — Role Assignment

The system shall allow authorized administrators to assign roles
to users.

### FR-USER-004 — Permission Enforcement

The system shall restrict protected operations according to the
permissions associated with the authenticated user's role.

---

## 4. Product Management

### FR-PROD-001 — Product Registration

The system shall allow authorized users to register products.

### FR-PROD-002 — Product Management

The system shall allow authorized users to consult, update and
deactivate products.

### FR-PROD-003 — Category Management

The system shall allow authorized users to manage product
categories.

### FR-PROD-004 — Product Availability

The system shall provide product availability information to
authorized users.

---

## 5. Customer Management

### FR-CUST-001 — Customer Registration

The system shall allow authorized users to register customers.

### FR-CUST-002 — Customer Management

The system shall allow authorized users to consult and update
customer information.

### FR-CUST-003 — Customer Association

The system shall allow a sale to be associated with a customer.

---

## 6. Supplier Management

### FR-SUP-001 — Supplier Registration

The system shall allow authorized users to register suppliers.

### FR-SUP-002 — Supplier Management

The system shall allow authorized users to consult and update
supplier information.

### FR-SUP-003 — Supplier Association

The system shall allow purchases to be associated with suppliers.

---

## 7. Inventory Management

### FR-INV-001 — Inventory Consultation

The system shall allow authorized users to consult current
inventory levels.

### FR-INV-002 — Inventory Movement

The system shall record inventory movements generated by
business operations.

### FR-INV-003 — Inventory Increase

The system shall increase inventory when a valid inventory-
increasing operation is completed.

### FR-INV-004 — Inventory Decrease

The system shall decrease inventory when a valid inventory-
decreasing operation is completed.

### FR-INV-005 — Stock Validation

The system shall validate product availability before completing
operations that decrease inventory.

### FR-INV-006 — Movement Traceability

The system shall maintain traceability for inventory movements.

---

## 8. Purchasing

### FR-PUR-001 — Purchase Registration

The system shall allow authorized users to register purchases.

### FR-PUR-002 — Purchase Items

The system shall allow a purchase to contain one or more products
with quantities and applicable prices.

### FR-PUR-003 — Purchase Supplier

The system shall associate each purchase with a supplier.

### FR-PUR-004 — Purchase Inventory Update

The system shall increase inventory after a valid purchase is
completed.

### FR-PUR-005 — Purchase Traceability

The system shall maintain the history of registered purchases.

---

## 9. Sales

### FR-SALE-001 — Sale Registration

The system shall allow authorized users to register sales.

### FR-SALE-002 — Sale Items

The system shall allow a sale to contain one or more products
with quantities and applicable prices.

### FR-SALE-003 — Sale Customer

The system shall associate a sale with a customer when required.

### FR-SALE-004 — Stock Availability

The system shall verify available inventory before completing
a sale.

### FR-SALE-005 — Sale Inventory Update

The system shall decrease inventory after a valid sale is
completed.

### FR-SALE-006 — Sale Traceability

The system shall maintain the history of registered sales.

---

## 10. Reporting

### FR-REP-001 — Sales Reporting

The system shall provide authorized users with sales information.

### FR-REP-002 — Inventory Reporting

The system shall provide authorized users with inventory
information.

### FR-REP-003 — Purchase Reporting

The system shall provide authorized users with purchase
information.

### FR-REP-004 — Operational Reporting

The system shall provide operational information required by
management.

---

## 11. Audit

### FR-AUD-001 — Audit Logging

The system shall record relevant security and business events.

### FR-AUD-002 — Audit Information

Audit records shall contain sufficient information to identify
the event, time and responsible user when applicable.

### FR-AUD-003 — Audit Consultation

Authorized users shall be able to consult audit records according
to their permissions.

---
